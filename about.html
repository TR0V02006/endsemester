About Our Organization

Kiwenda Capital SACCO is an organization established to promote a culture of saving and financial responsibility among individuals and communities.

Our Mission

To empower people through accessible savings services, financial education and responsible financial support.

Our Vision

To become a trusted savings organization that promotes financial stability and economic development.

Our Objectives

  • Encourage people to develop a saving culture.
  • Promote financial literacy among communities.
  • Support members in achieving their financial goals.
  • Provide responsible access to financial services.
  • Promote economic empowerment and development.
